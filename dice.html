How to play

The pay table

Place a different M-Points wager on each total you select. All selected bets are taken from your balance each roll.

  • 2 or 12 1 way in 3612× back
  • 3 2 ways in 366× back
  • 4 or 10 3 ways in 364× back
  • 5 or 9 4 ways in 363× back
  • 6 or 8 5 ways in 362× back
  • Match on doubles Same number on both diceDouble payout
  • Hit a selected numberWin its payout
  • Miss a selected number Except 7 or 11Lose that wager
  • Roll 7 or 11Lose all bets + new game

A win pays only the wager on the matching total; other selected wagers lose. “Back” includes the winning wager. Matching dice double that bet's payout. Rolling 7 or 11 loses every selected wager and starts a new game. M-Points have no cash value.
