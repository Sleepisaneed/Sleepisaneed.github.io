How to play

The pay table

Choose one total and wager points on each roll. Match your number to win its payout; a different safe total loses your wager but the game continues.

  • Bet on 2 (snake eyes)2× back
  • Bet on 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, or 124× back
  • Roll a number other than your betLose wager
  • Roll 7 or 11Lose + new game

On a match, “back” includes your wager. Rolling 7 or 11 ends the current run and starts a new game. M-Points have no cash value.
